About

My academic background combines fisheries and aquaculture with graduate training in seafood processing. My work spans controlled aquaculture trials, fish ecology and physiology, seafood quality assessment, post-harvest processing, and protein-focused analytical research.

I currently work at the University of Tehran as a Research and Lab Assistant, supporting field studies, laboratory operations, student instruction, experimental design, and thesis research in aquaculture technology and seafood quality.

Selected work

Research projects

Aquaculture and fish biology

University of Tehran · 2025–ongoing

Anti-stress agents under high-temperature stress in Beluga (Huso huso)

Advisor and experimental designer for work examining combined astaxanthin, vitamin E, and selenium. Designed 16 independent RAS systems and supported fish acclimation, maintenance, troubleshooting, sampling, and data analysis.

University of Tehran · 2024–2025

Arginine, vitamin C, and zinc under hypoxia-induced stress in rainbow trout

Advisor and experimental designer. The study used 12 independent RAS systems and measured growth, stress, and immune indices including glucose, cortisol, total antioxidants, peroxidase, and ACH50.

Islamic Azad University, Qaemshahr Branch · 2025–ongoing

Ganoderma lucidum in Siberian sturgeon diets

Supported a 60-day feeding trial with three dietary concentrations. Conducted blood and liver sampling and assessed growth performance, immune markers, catalase, and superoxide dismutase activity.

2023–present

Freshwater ecological studies

Field studies on rainbow trout and brown trout in Tar and Lar Lakes, pike in Ovan Lake, and brown trout in the Chalus River, covering population traits, morphology, behavior, feeding habits, and environmental quality parameters.

Seafood processing and quality

University of Tehran · 2025–2026

Ultrasound thawing and myofibrillar protein integrity in common carp

Compared ultrasound, water-immersion, and microwave thawing. Evaluated fillet and protein structure using methods including AFM, SEM, TPA, color analysis, DLS, zeta potential, and SDS-PAGE.

Open publication DOI
University of Tehran · 2024–2025

Sous-vide and Shirazi thyme extract in whiteleg shrimp

Advisor and experimental designer. Assessed microbiological, chemical, physical, color, texture, and sensory quality parameters to study shelf life and product quality.

University of Tehran · 2024–2025

Hydrolyzed collagen from Nile tilapia skin

Advisor and experimental designer. Extracted collagen and hydrolyzed it using Trichoderma ghanense (DH ≈ 27%). FT-IR and DSC were used to assess structural and thermal characteristics.
